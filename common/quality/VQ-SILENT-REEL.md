# VQ-SILENT-REEL — Silent-First Readability

Most reels in this workspace should work without recorded voice. Silence is a
production constraint, not a reason to turn the reel into fast presentation
slides. The image carries the proof; text only supplies the missing causal
link.

## Use when

- A reel has no spoken VO, or must remain understandable with sound off.
- Text, gameplay, references, and annotations compete for attention.
- Feedback says the reel is too fast, requires replay to understand, or does
  not clearly show the final result.

## Do not use for

- A voiced micro-story where captions primarily mirror speech. Use the normal
  caption, hook, safe-zone, and motion rules.
- Long tutorials whose purpose is reading detailed instructions.

## Check

- Default length is **8–15 seconds**, built from **3–4 shots** around one idea.
- Show no more than **one focal object** and **one short message** at a time.
- Use at most **three audience-facing messages** across the reel. Prefer
  2–6 words each; every message remains readable for at least **2 seconds**.
- The first 0.5s names or visually demonstrates the problem/contrast.
- Gameplay/product proof is large enough to read on a phone. Paper notes,
  arrows, and generated references support it; they do not shrink or replace
  the proof.
- For an iteration story, preserve the causal chain:
  `variants/problem -> selected reference -> match cut -> in-product result`.
  The selected reference and implemented result should share framing or shape
  so the relationship is understood without narration.
- Hold the resolved result deliberately for about **1 second**. Do not end
  while the final state is still entering or before the last variant is clear.
- “Slow it down” is first solved by removing simultaneous details, not by
  doubling every shot. A viewer should understand the reel in one play.
- Music and SFX may carry rhythm, but comprehension must not depend on sound.

## Evidence required

- The exact MP4 plus its review pack.
- A list of all audience-facing messages and their measured screen durations.
- One uninformed-viewer answer to the reel's factual question, for example:
  “Which design was selected, and did it reach the game?”
- For a silent reel with gameplay: a clean `VQ-TEMPORAL` preflight.

## Not enough

- High replay count by itself. Interested viewers may replay; confused viewers
  may swipe away.
- “It is only ten seconds” without measuring how many things must be read.
- Adding VO as the default repair. VO consumes author time and is reserved for
  selected stories.
- Forcing color because one viewer disliked monochrome. Palette is a style
  choice; subject/background contrast and product visibility are the gate.
