# VQ-HOOK — Goal-in-First-Second

The viewer can answer "what is this about?" from voice within the first
second. No code gate — this is judged from the transcript and the actual
delivered audio, not the text overlay.

## Use when

- Writing, recording, or reviewing the opening line of a reel/short.
- Reviewing a devlog's cold-open beat.
- A reviewer is deciding whether an opening needs a rewrite or re-record
  before any visual polish is worth doing.

## Do not use for

- Mid-video build/climax beats once the hook has already landed — pacing
  and hierarchy rules (see `HIT_VIDEO_PRACTICES.md`'s Visual Hierarchy
  section) apply there instead.
- Judging visual polish on a beat whose VO opening is still unresolved —
  fix the hook first; visual fixes on a broken hook are wasted effort.

## Check

- First spoken sentence names the product, problem, or situation — if the
  viewer cannot answer "what is this about?" after one second, the VO needs
  a rewrite or re-record, not a visual fix.
- The opening is voice-driven, not dependent on a text overlay to explain
  context.
- Opens on a viewer-facing problem, contradiction, funny situation,
  concrete number, or visible failure — not a neutral feature tour, and not
  what the author personally finds interesting.
- Opens on a specific number or noun ("30 000 СТРОК"), not a category ("о
  чём игра").
- No dependency on a prior reel: avoid openings like "а", "можно",
  "теперь" unless the product/context was already named in this reel's
  voice.
- VO energy is conversational and interested, not flat/read-on-autopilot —
  a technically clean take with LRA in range can still fail this if the
  delivery sounds bored (see VQ-AUDIO for the LRA numbers; this rule is the
  judgment call on top of them).
- Face beats: 0.3-0.5s of held eye contact at the head of the clip before
  the first cut, so it reads as direct address, not B-roll voice-over.

## Evidence required

- The actual first spoken sentence, quoted, with its timestamp.
- LRA/loudness numbers for the opening line (from VQ-AUDIO) as supporting
  evidence for the energy judgment, not a replacement for listening to it.
- A frame at the opening timestamp for face beats (eye-contact check).

## Not enough

- "Hook feels strong" without quoting the actual first line.
- Judging the hook from the text overlay while ignoring what the VO
  actually says.
- Passing a technically-clean-but-flat take because the loudness numbers
  are in range — LRA in range plus bored delivery is still a re-record.
